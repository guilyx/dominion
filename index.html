Objectives

    Controls (H to hide)

    • Left-drag on land: pan. Right-drag: rotate. Wheel: zoom. WASD/QE too.
    • Click a villager, tree, rock or heap to pick it up. Release to drop; flick to throw.
    • Drop a villager on a field, house, altar, armoury, construction site, tree or rock to give them that job.
    • Drop wood/ore/food on your temple to store it. Drop food on your creature to feed it.
    • Stroke your creature (hold and move) to reward, tap it to punish.
    • Miracles: number keys or the Miracles tab, then click. Hold Shift to keep casting.
    • R rotates a building ghost. Right-click cancels. Space centres on your temple. F finds your creature.
    • F5 quick save, F9 quick load. P pause.
